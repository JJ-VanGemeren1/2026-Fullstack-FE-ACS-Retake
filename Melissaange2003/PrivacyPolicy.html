Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Who We Are

Cytek is a Belgian electronics resale company based in Geel, 2440. We buy and sell used electronics at fair prices. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us or fill in our trade-in form, we may collect the following:

. Your full name

. Your email address

. Information about the device you want to sell

. Basic usage data such as pages visited and browser type

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your trade-in requests, contact you about your device, and improve our website and services. We do not sell your data to anyone.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal data with third parties except when required to deliver our services. We never sell your information to advertisers or marketing companies.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To do so, email us at info@cytek.com with the subject "Data Request." We will respond within 30 days.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us at info@cytek.com
