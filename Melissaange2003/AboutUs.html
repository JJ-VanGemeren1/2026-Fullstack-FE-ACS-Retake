Who We Are

Cytek is a Belgian electronics resale company. We started because we saw how many perfectly good devices end up in a drawer or worse, in the bin.

We collect used electronics, test them properly, and put them back on the market at fair prices. You can buy from us, or sell us your old gear. Simple.

We accept computers, laptops, headphones, cameras, Raspberry Pi boards, phones, tablets, and much more.
